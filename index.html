How SILAH Works

A simple two-way flow: family sends a reminder, the grandparent responds, and the family receives the acknowledgement.

1

Create

Family member creates a reminder.

2

Send

Personal voice, AI voice, or text.

3

Receive

Grandparent gets the notification.

4

Reply

Voice, “I Took It”, or text.

5

Confirm

Sender receives the acknowledgement.
